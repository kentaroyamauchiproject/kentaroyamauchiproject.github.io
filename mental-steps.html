Mental Steps

Something to take when your mind needs to move.

Mental Steps are guided steps that help your mind move from where it is now toward where you want it to be next.

They can happen inside MINDBEBOP ecosystem, outside MINDBEBOP, or both.

What Are Mental Steps?

Sometimes you know where you want to be next, but your mind is not there yet.

You may know what needs to happen and still hesitate, loop, freeze, lose momentum, or feel unable to begin.

Mental Steps give you something concrete to take next.

A set of Mental Steps may include:

  • A real-world action
  • A question
  • Something to notice
  • Something to write
  • A pause
  • A MINDBEBOP app
  • Another tool or app
  • A different route when the current one is not working

It does not matter where the step happens.

What matters is whether it helps the navigation continue.

Mental Steps and Mental Navigation

Mental Navigation is about helping your mind move from Point A toward Point B.

Point A
Where your mind is now

↓

Mental Steps

↓

Point B
Where you want your mind to be next

Mental Steps are not the destination.

They are what you take along the way.

They Do Not Have to Use MINDBEBOP apps

Some Mental Steps use MINDBEBOP apps.

Open MindZoneOut for 60 seconds.
Return to the current step.
Continue.

Some Mental Steps use ordinary actions.

Stand up.
Walk to the desk.
Open the document.

Others combine both.

MINDBEBOP does not need to be part of every step.

The point is to help your mind keep moving.

MindEntry Holds the Steps

A useful method can still create friction if you have to remember the whole sequence.

MindEntry can hold the Mental Steps for you and show them one at a time.

You do not need to keep the sequence in your head.

You only need to deal with what comes next.

One screen.
One step.
Then the next.

Fixed Mental Steps

Some Mental Steps follow the same route every time.

Open the document.
Write one sentence.
Save the file.

When the same sequence works, a fixed route may be enough.

Stateful Mental Steps

Other Mental Steps can respond to what is happening during the journey.

The destination can stay the same while the route changes.

One destination.
Different routes.

Learn about Stateful Mental Steps

Example: The First Move

You know what needs to be done, but you cannot begin.

Instead of asking you to complete the whole task, Mental Steps can reduce the distance of the next move.

Point B:
Write the report

Next step:
Open the document

The destination can remain large.

The next step does not have to be.

Start With the Moment You Are In

You do not need to understand the whole MINDBEBOP system first.

Start with what is happening now.

Your mind may be:

  • Looping
  • Unable to start
  • Overloaded
  • Waiting
  • Restless
  • Still carrying something from earlier

Then take the Mental Steps that fit that moment.

Where is your mind now?

Where do you want it to be next?

Take Mental Steps.
