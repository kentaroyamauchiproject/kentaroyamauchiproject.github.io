Cross-Dimensional Handshake

The bidirectional connection between the physical world and the Mental Background OS.

Most MINDBEBOP tools exist entirely inside the Mental Background Layer. Thoughts are flipped, shouted, released, protected, or guided through recipes without ever leaving that space.

A Cross-Dimensional Handshake is different.

Rather than existing as an isolated app, MINDBEBOP becomes a bridge where real-world events influence mental state movement, and mental state movement can, in turn, influence the real world.

The Bidirectional Circle

Reality-driven inputs enter MindEntry as secure MindEvents, while completed Recipes, CogniHacks, and other meaningful mental transitions emit quiet webhook outputs that trigger actions in the external world.

Webhook Outputs to the Real World

Incoming: Reality-Driven Inputs

Reality can now securely wake up your execution environments without requiring active mental navigation. Through native Apple integration and system intercepts, external triggers inject directly into the OS layer:

  • ShareImport Extension: Select raw text or thought residue in any external application (Web browsers, Mail, Notes, Documents) and safely offload it straight into the MindEntry stream.
  • URL Schemes & Native App Intents: Launch specific recipes instantly from system hardware shortcuts, custom automation widgets, or deep links embedded in your local workflows.
  • Native Shortcuts Integration: Route calendar events, location transitions (e.g., arriving home), or physical NFC tags to trigger automated context injection.

Outgoing: Webhook Emissions

When a mental transition reaches its end, MindEntry securely notifies your custom endpoints. The app’s role ends at sending the event—it remains an objective emmiter rather than an accountability or configuration layer.

  • Turn on smart workspace lights
  • Switch system focus modes or playlists
  • Update your remote communication statuses
  • Log execution timestamps to personal analytical sheets
  • Trigger custom routines across Zapier, Make, n8n, or Home Assistant

The Firewall Principle

MINDBEBOP does not automate your life, nor does it let external chaos corrupt your internal silence.

Only the clean metadata of structural transitions (such as recipe_started or recipe_completed) passes through the boundary. Your raw thoughts, private logs, and internal reflections never leave the absolute privacy of the local device layer.
