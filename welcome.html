Welcome. We're glad you're here.

If your mind has ever kept running something after you wanted your attention somewhere else, you're in the right place.

MINDBEBOP is a small, calm set of noise-canceling apps for your mind — for thoughts that keep replaying, waiting, checking, returning, asking for attention, or simply refusing to close.

A reply you're waiting for. A task you already know you need to do. A memory that keeps replaying. A distraction you keep returning to. A thought you don't want to deal with right now.

Not every thought is a problem. Not every thought needs to be fixed. But sometimes you want to do something with one.

MINDBEBOP gives you small tools for those moments: look at a thought differently, give it another time, create some distance, give it a place, take a Mental Step, or simply leave it alone for now.

No streaks. No scores. No pressure to process everything.

Not another To Do app

A To Do app helps you keep track of what needs to be done.

But sometimes you already know what needs to be done.

You just aren't there yet.

Maybe your mind is still at work when you're already home. Maybe the task is sitting right there on your To Do list, but starting feels weirdly unavailable. Maybe you know you want to stop checking your phone, but your attention keeps going back.

MINDBEBOP is not a replacement for your To Do app. It works on a different question:

Where is your mind now — and where do you want it to go?

Reduce. Navigate. Anchor.

MINDBEBOP tools can help in three broad ways.

Reduce what keeps asking for attention.

Navigate when there is somewhere you want to go.

Anchor something from an arrival worth remembering.

These are possibilities, not steps you have to follow. A thought may need one of them, several of them, or none at all.

Start with MindEntry

Mental Navigation, one step at a time.

If you know there is somewhere you want to go but getting there feels difficult, MindEntry can help you begin.

Mental Navigation starts with your Current Position: where your mind actually is now.

Then you choose where you want to go. That is your Point B.

Mental Steps can help you move toward it one step at a time.

A Mental Step may open another MINDBEBOP tool. Or it may guide a real-world action: put the phone down, walk outside, wash one dish, make coffee, breathe, stretch, cook, or simply stop for a moment.

You choose Point B. The route can change.

Or give a thought somewhere to be

A thought doesn't need a destination yet.

Sometimes you have a thought without knowing what you want to do with it.

In MindBackyard's Thought Space, you can turn a selected thought into a persistent Thought Block and give it a place.

Move it. Approach it. Rotate it. Change your perspective. Or leave it where it is.

A Thought Block can also connect to one or more possible Mental Steps. If you later decide there is somewhere you want to go, you can choose a path and continue in MindEntry.

A thought can have a place before it has a destination.

Possibility is not pressure.

MindBlank

Sometimes nothing needs to come next.

Most software measures engagement.

MindBlank looks in the other direction: time spent away from active MINDBEBOP interaction in supported situations.

It does not claim to know whether your mind was literally blank or what you were thinking during that time.

It is not a score, streak, diagnosis, or performance metric.

The Apps

Different moments may call for different tools. Use one by itself, or move between them as part of a Mental Navigation.

MindEntry

App size: ~4.0 MB
Available on the App Store — free
/subscription/purchase

Mental Navigation. For getting from where you are toward where you want to go.

Take the Next Move. MindEntry runs Mental Steps one step at a time. A route can move through MINDBEBOP apps, real-world actions, or both — and can change as your Current Position changes.

MindBackyard

App size: ~3.1 MB
Available on the App Store — free

Thought Space and digital fidget space.

Give thoughts or attention somewhere to move. MindBackyard includes two different environments. Infinite Hallway gives restless attention an open-ended place to wander. Thought Space lets selected thoughts become persistent Thought Blocks that you can place, approach, move, rotate, resize, and connect with possible Mental Steps.

MindFlipOut

App size: ~1.9 MB
Available on the App Store — free

Thought reframing. For thoughts that keep replaying.

Look at it differently. MindFlipOut lets you capture a recurring thought and look at it again from another perspective.

MindShoutOut

App size: ~2.4 MB
Available on the App Store — free

Reminders. For things you don't want to forget.

Give it another time. MindShoutOut lets you schedule a thought to return later instead of requiring you to keep remembering it right now.

MindZoneOut

App size: ~2.0 MB
Available on the App Store — free

Mental break. For moments when you want less stimulation.

Zone out for a moment. MindZoneOut provides a simple, low-stimulation space where you can pause without needing to accomplish anything.

MindBackOut

App size: ~2.2 MB
Available on the App Store — free

App blocking. For creating distance from digital distractions.

Back out for a while. MindBackOut lets you temporarily block selected apps. It can also protect compatible Mental Steps from selected digital distractions while a Mental Navigation is active.
