Welcome. We're glad you're here.

If your mind has ever felt like it's still running something in the background, you're in the right place.

MINDBEBOP is a small, calm set of apps for creating an intentional relationship with thoughts that would otherwise be left unattended.

Sometimes a thought needs less attention. Sometimes it needs somewhere to go. Sometimes it just needs somewhere to be.

Some thoughts don't become problems.

They just stay active.

A reply you're waiting for. A task you keep reopening. A memory that keeps replaying. A distraction you keep returning to. A thought that never seems to fully leave.

Different thoughts may need different relationships.

You might look at one differently. Set another aside until later. Step away from something competing for your attention. Give your mind a quiet moment. Give a thought a place in space. Or begin moving toward somewhere you want to go from it.

MINDBEBOP provides small tools for those different relationships — without requiring every thought to become a task, goal, or problem to solve.

No streaks. No coaching. No pressure. Just different ways to relate to what's active in your mind.

Start with MindEntry

The entry point to MINDBEBOP’s Mental Background OS.

Not sure what to do next? That's exactly what MindEntry is for.

Start with what is active. Then follow one simple step at a time.

Mental Steps may open another MINDBEBOP tool. Or they may guide a real-world action: put the phone down, breathe, walk outside, wash one dish, make coffee, or simply stop for a moment.

The goal isn't productivity. It's Mental Navigation: helping you move from your Current Position toward a Mental Destination, one move at a time.

And if the route needs to change along the way, it can.

MindBlank

The quiet after a thought no longer needs your attention.

Most software measures engagement.

MINDBEBOP is interested in something else too: the moment you no longer need to keep checking, replaying, carrying, managing, or holding a thought in immediate attention.

That quiet space is called MindBlank.

The Modules

Each module provides a different way to relate to thoughts and attention.

MindEntry

App size: ~4.0 MB
Available on the App Store — free
/subscription/purchase

Mental Steps. For moments when you're stuck.

Start with what is active. MindEntry helps you move toward where you want to be through Mental Steps — one move at a time. A route can include real-world actions or other MINDBEBOP tools, and it can change as your Current Position changes.

MindBackyard

App size: ~3.1 MB
Available on the App Store — free

Thought Space and digital fidgeting. For thoughts and restless attention.

Give attention somewhere to move — or give a thought somewhere to be. MindBackyard lets you wander through low-stimulation environments without a goal, or enter Thought Space and give thoughts persistent places in 3D space.

MINDBEBOP calls this Spatial Mental Shelving: set a thought aside as a Thought Block, leave it somewhere, and return when you want.

If there is somewhere you might want to go from that thought, you can also connect one or more Mental Steps and keep those possible paths available without turning the thought into a task.

Explore MindBackyard →

MindFlipOut

App size: ~1.9 MB
Available on the App Store — free

Thought reframing. For thoughts that keep replaying.

Respond to looping thoughts. Catch a thought when it appears and respond (flip) with another way of looking at it. MindFlipOut gives you a way to intentionally change your relationship with a recurring thought instead of simply replaying it.

MindShoutOut

App size: ~2.4 MB
Available on the App Store — free

Reminders. For things you don't want to forget.

Set it aside. Some thoughts matter, but they do not need to stay in immediate attention. MindShoutOut lets you schedule a thought to return (shout) later, so you do not have to keep carrying it right now.

You’re not losing the thought.
You’re giving it another time to return.

MindZoneOut

App size: ~2.0 MB
Available on the App Store — free

Mental break. For mental overload.

Give your mind a quiet moment. Step away from noise and sit with a quiet screen designed to create a pause. MindZoneOut gives attention somewhere quieter to be without requiring you to solve or accomplish anything.

MindBackOut

App size: ~2.2 MB
Available on the App Store — free

App blocking. For staying away from distractions.

Create some distance. MindBackOut lets you temporarily block selected apps when you want some space from them. It can also work with Mental Steps in MindEntry, helping protect the route you chose from selected distractions.

Sometimes a thought needs to quiet down.
Sometimes it needs a place.
Sometimes there is somewhere you want to go from it.
