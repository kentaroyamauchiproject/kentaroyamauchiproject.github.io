MINDBEBOP FAQ

Last Updated: 2026-09-17

What is MINDBEBOP?

MINDBEBOP is a Mental Background OS built from small, independent apps for creating an intentional relationship with thoughts that would otherwise be left unattended.

Rather than trying to become one application that does everything, MINDBEBOP uses lightweight tools that each offer a different way of interacting with a thought — looking at it differently, giving it another time to return, creating some distance, giving it a place, or navigating toward somewhere you want to go.

Mental hygiene remains the philosophy. Mental Navigation is one part of the system for moments when there is somewhere you want to go.

What is a Mental Background OS?

A Mental Background OS is MINDBEBOP’s name for a modular system designed to support the Mental Background layer — the part of everyday mental life where thoughts can remain present while your attention is somewhere else.

It does not replace your thinking or tell you what to think. It provides lightweight ways to intentionally relate to thoughts — including reducing Thought Noise, setting something aside, giving a thought a place, changing perspective, or beginning Mental Navigation when you choose a destination.

Each app performs one small role. Together they form a modular environment for everyday mental life.

What is Mental Navigation?

Mental Navigation is the process of intentionally moving from your Current Position toward a Mental Destination.

It asks: “Where am I now, and where do I want to go from here?”

Mental Steps can provide a path for that journey. The path can continue, pause, or change as you move.

Not every thought needs Mental Navigation. Sometimes changing your relationship with a thought, giving it a place, or simply leaving it alone is enough.

Is MINDBEBOP therapy or a medical product?

No. MINDBEBOP is not therapy, medical treatment, or a diagnostic tool.

MINDBEBOP is about everyday relationships with thoughts — noticing them, interacting with them intentionally, giving them somewhere to remain, or navigating from them when you choose.

Our apps are designed as everyday tools for thought care — similar to writing a thought down, looking at it differently, taking a quiet pause, setting something aside, or deciding what you want to do next.

MINDBEBOP does not diagnose conditions, provide treatment, or replace professional mental health care.

Do you have a psychology or medical background?

MINDBEBOP was created from lived experience, observation, experimentation, and a desire to build simple, human tools for everyday relationships with thoughts.

While we respect and value professional mental health disciplines, MINDBEBOP is intentionally designed to be non-clinical and non-therapeutic.

Who are these apps for?

MINDBEBOP is for people who want a lighter, more intentional relationship with their thoughts — without turning it into a performance, diagnosis, or long routine.

You don’t need prior experience with meditation or journaling. A thought can be noticed, answered, set aside, given a place, revisited later, or used as the beginning of Mental Navigation. You decide what relationship makes sense at the moment.

What do you mean by “Thought Noise”?

Thought Noise is MINDBEBOP’s name for thoughts that keep drawing attention even when you would rather your attention be somewhere else.

It isn't a diagnosis or a medical term. It describes an everyday experience rather than making a judgment about whether a thought is important, meaningful, or something that needs to be eliminated.

Thought Noise can take many forms: something you keep repeating to yourself, something you are waiting for, an unfinished thought, a reminder that keeps returning, or mental activity that simply feels louder than you want it to be right now.

Sometimes changing your relationship with the thought is enough. You might look at it differently, give it another time to return, create some distance, or give your attention a quiet place to move. Sometimes the thought remains. If so, you can leave it alone, give it a place in Thought Space, or begin Mental Navigation if you decide there is somewhere you want to go from it.

Want to explore the idea further? See Thought Noise.

What is Thought Space?

Thought Space is a spatial environment inside MindBackyard where selected thoughts can exist as persistent Thought Blocks.

A Thought Block does not have to become a task, goal, or Mental Destination. It can simply have a place and remain available until you choose to approach it again.

Thought Space can also keep possible Mental Steps connected with a Thought Block without requiring you to begin them.

Thought Space is not intended to create a complete map of everything in your mind. You choose which thoughts, if any, you want to place there.

Explore Thought Space.

What is Spatial Mental Shelving?

Spatial Mental Shelving is MINDBEBOP’s name for making the act of setting a thought aside spatial.

Instead of needing to keep a thought in immediate attention, you can give it a visible, persistent place in Thought Space and return to it when you want.

The “shelving” is the act of setting something aside mentally. It does not require a literal shelf or a particular kind of Thought Furniture.

What is MindEaseOut? Is it just avoidance?

MindEaseOut is a function for intentionally changing your relationship with a thought in relation to time.

It does not declare the thought false, erase it, or decide what the thought means. It provides a way to acknowledge that a thought may relate to another time rather than requiring your attention in the same way right now.

Whether setting something aside is useful depends on the thought and the situation. MINDBEBOP does not decide that for you.

MindEaseOut is a function within the broader MINDBEBOP architecture, not a separate app.

How often should I use the apps?

There is no required frequency.

You can use the apps daily, occasionally, or only when a thought comes up. There are no streaks you need to maintain and no required routine.

How is MINDBEBOP different from journaling or wellness apps?

Journaling often gives thoughts somewhere to be written. MINDBEBOP extends that idea by giving you different ways to interact with what you have noticed.

A thought might be looked at from another perspective, given another time to return, placed in Thought Space, connected with possible Mental Steps, or used as the beginning of Mental Navigation. Or you can leave it alone.

The aim is not to build a record of everything you think. It is to give you simple ways to create an intentional relationship with a thought when you want one.

If you’re curious how people use the apps in everyday situations, you can see a few quiet examples here: Everyday moments.

Is my data private?

MINDBEBOP is designed primarily around local, on-device processing and storage. Your thought content is not automatically uploaded to MINDBEBOP.

MINDBEBOP apps can communicate with one another locally on your device to continue supported experiences. That internal MINDBEBOP communication is not Cross-Dimensional Handshake.

Cross-Dimensional Handshake is optional communication across the boundary between MINDBEBOP and the outside world. Outbound Cross-Dimensional Handshake events can send limited technical event metadata to an HTTPS destination you configure. They do not transmit user-created text.

MindEvent is an inbound Cross-Dimensional Handshake: it allows supported information or actions from outside MINDBEBOP to enter the MINDBEBOP environment.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

What are the individual apps?

MindEntry is the Mental Steps and Mental Navigation layer of MINDBEBOP. It lets you begin, continue, adapt, pause, and return to Mental Navigation.

MindFlipOut gives you a way to look at a recurring thought from another perspective.

MindShoutOut lets you give a thought another time to return through reminders and related actions.

MindZoneOut creates short periods of intentional quiet and reduced stimulation.

MindBackyard provides environments for attention and thoughts, including Infinite Hallway and Thought Space.

MindBackOut helps create intentional distance from selected apps and can provide App Blocking during Mental Steps.

You can use one app by itself or move between multiple apps when a Mental Step or another action calls for a different kind of interaction.

What are Mental Steps?

Mental Steps are paths you can follow during Mental Navigation.

A set of Mental Steps can help you move from your Current Position toward a Mental Destination through a sequence of intentional moves.

Some Mental Steps can be completed entirely within MindEntry. Others may open supporting MINDBEBOP apps when a different kind of action or environment is useful.

Mental Steps are guides, not commands. You can continue, pause, change course, or leave the Navigation.

Can a Thought Block have Mental Steps?

Yes. A Thought Block in Thought Space can be connected with one or more saved sets of Mental Steps.

These connections represent possible paths, not obligations. The Thought Block remains a thought rather than becoming a task or destination.

When you want to go somewhere from that thought, you can choose one of its connected Mental Steps and begin it in MindEntry.

You can also leave the Thought Block exactly where it is and do nothing with those paths.

Why are there multiple apps instead of one?

Different moments can call for different ways of relating to a thought.

Rather than combining every feature into one large application, MINDBEBOP keeps each tool independent.

This makes every app simpler to understand, easier to use, and easier to ignore when you don’t need it.

What is Hallway Mode?

Hallway Mode presents a set of Mental Steps as movement through a quiet hallway instead of a traditional step list.

It offers an alternative way of experiencing Mental Navigation without changing the Mental Steps themselves.

What is Infinite Hallway?

Infinite Hallway is an environment in MindBackyard where you can move and wander without needing a goal, score, or destination.

It is separate from Thought Space. Thought Space gives selected thoughts places; Infinite Hallway gives attention somewhere to move.

Infinite Hallway can also be used as part of Mental Steps. A Mental Step may send you there for a while and then return you to the same active Mental Navigation.

What is MindAnchor?

MindAnchor lets you optionally preserve something from the state you reached through a successful Mental Navigation.

It can help you return to something meaningful from that arrival after everyday life moves on.

What is Thought Furniture?

Thought Furniture is an experimental idea for structures inside Thought Space that could change how you encounter or interact with a thought.

Thought Furniture might reveal, conceal, separate, combine, reorient, transform, or provide another perspective on thoughts. The important part is not what the object looks like, but what relationship with a thought it makes possible.

Thought Furniture is not the same as Spatial Mental Shelving. Spatial Mental Shelving gives a thought a place. Thought Furniture explores what else might happen when you encounter a thought through a particular structure.

This is an area MINDBEBOP is exploring, not a claim about how the mind works.

What is Cross-Dimensional Handshake?

Cross-Dimensional Handshake is MINDBEBOP’s architecture for communication across the boundary between MINDBEBOP and the outside world.

A Cross-Dimensional Handshake can be outbound or inbound.

An outbound Cross-Dimensional Handshake allows supported MINDBEBOP events to send limited event information to an external HTTPS destination you configure. What happens next is defined by you and the external system.

An inbound Cross-Dimensional Handshake allows supported information or actions from outside MINDBEBOP to enter the MINDBEBOP environment.

Communication between one MINDBEBOP app and another MINDBEBOP app on your device is not Cross-Dimensional Handshake. That is internal on-device communication within MINDBEBOP.

Outbound Cross-Dimensional Handshake transmits events, not thoughts. It does not transmit user-created text such as thought text, Mental Steps names or instructions, Mental Destinations, Current Positions, Next Moves, reminders, reflections, or Thought Block text.

What is MindEvent API?

MindEvent is an inbound Cross-Dimensional Handshake.

It provides a way for supported information or actions from the outside world to enter MINDBEBOP and initiate a supported experience.

This is the opposite direction from an outbound Cross-Dimensional Handshake, where a supported MINDBEBOP event crosses outward to an external destination.

Neither direction turns communication between MINDBEBOP apps themselves into Cross-Dimensional Handshake. Internal app-to-app communication remains inside the MINDBEBOP environment.

App-Specific FAQs

What does MindEntry actually do?

MindEntry is where Mental Steps and Mental Navigation can run.

You can begin with a Mental Destination, follow Mental Steps, move into another MINDBEBOP tool when a step calls for it, return to the Navigation, pause it, change course, or continue later.

The goal is not to analyze you or decide where you should go. You choose the destination and remain in control of the Navigation.

Are Mental Steps automatic?

No.

Mental Steps can guide movement between actions and apps, but you remain in control of the Navigation. You can continue, pause, change course, or leave it.

A possible path does not become an obligation simply because MINDBEBOP makes it available.

I used MindFlipOut and the thought didn’t disappear. Does it actually work?

MindFlipOut does not erase thoughts.

It gives you a way to respond to a recurring thought and look at it from another perspective. The relationship with the thought may change even if the thought itself remains.

Sometimes that change may be enough for the moment. Sometimes the thought remains present. MINDBEBOP does not assume that every thought should disappear.

Is MindFlipOut just forcing positive thinking?

No.

Heavy and Light describe different ways of experiencing or responding to a thought — not whether a thought is morally good or bad.

A Light response does not have to be cheerful or unrealistically positive. It can simply offer another way of seeing or responding to what is already there.

The purpose is perspective, not denial.

What does “Acknowledge” actually do in MindShoutOut?

It prevents passive dismissal.

When Acknowledge is enabled, a shout must be consciously confirmed.

The thought is delivered. You mark it as received. What you do with it after that is up to you.

What does “When Tapped” mean in MindShoutOut?

“When Tapped” lets you choose what happens when you tap a shout notification.

Depending on your selection, the tap can open MindShoutOut, MindEntry, or MindZoneOut.

This makes the reminder a possible doorway into another interaction rather than requiring every shout to end in the same place.

Why not just use a standard reminder app?

You can.

MindShoutOut is designed around the relationship with the thought behind the reminder: you can give it another time to return instead of continuing to carry it in immediate attention.

It is not intended to turn every reminder into a productivity task.

Why use MindZoneOut instead of just closing my eyes?

You may not need to.

MindZoneOut provides a defined beginning, a quiet space, and a clear ending. That small amount of structure can provide a deliberate period with less stimulation.

If closing your eyes already gives you what you want, that may be enough.

What is MindBackyard for?

MindBackyard provides spaces where attention and thoughts can exist without everything becoming a task.

Infinite Hallway gives attention somewhere to wander. Thought Space lets selected thoughts exist as persistent Thought Blocks through Spatial Mental Shelving.

You can move without a destination, approach a thought when you want, pass it by, or begin Mental Navigation from it if you eventually decide there is somewhere you want to go.

Is MindBackyard a game?

Not in the usual sense.

It can be playful, but it is not built around winning, scores, streaks, clearing everything, or completing a board.

It is an environment for attention and thoughts rather than a system that asks you to perform.

Is MindBackOut about controlling myself?

It is about creating intentional distance from selected apps.

You choose what to block and when to use that distance. MindBackOut can also provide App Blocking during Mental Steps when you choose that support.

The app does not judge what you should or should not use.

Will MINDBEBOP make my thoughts disappear?

No.

MINDBEBOP is not built around making thoughts disappear. It gives you different ways to create an intentional relationship with them.

A thought might become quieter, remain where you placed it, return later, look different from another perspective, or become the starting point for Mental Navigation.

Sometimes nothing needs to happen to the thought at all.

When should I seek professional help?

MINDBEBOP is designed for everyday mental hygiene and interaction with thoughts — not for crisis support, diagnosis, or treatment.

If thoughts are overwhelming, persistent, intrusive, or interfere with daily functioning, consider consulting a qualified mental health professional.

If you are experiencing severe distress or are in crisis, contact local emergency services or an appropriate qualified professional.

MINDBEBOP is not a substitute for professional mental health care.
