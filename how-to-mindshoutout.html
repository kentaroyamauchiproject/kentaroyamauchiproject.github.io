How to Use MindShoutOut

Available on the App Store

Your mind is full of open loops. Something important to remember. Details for your next meeting. A good idea you don't want to lose.

You don't have to keep holding onto it in your mind. Enter it into MindShoutOut, then decide whether it needs to come back to your attention.

Toss it to save it without a reminder. Schedule it if you need one. Put it on your calendar when it belongs there.

Keep the information. Free up your attention.

STEP 1 Capture What's on Your Mind

Open MindShoutOut and enter something you want to keep: a meeting detail, a good idea, or anything you don't want to forget.

You can decide what to do with it next.

STEP 2 Toss It — No Reminder Needed

If the information doesn't need a reminder, choose Toss. MindShoutOut saves it; Toss does not delete it.

You can return to the saved information later. You don't have to schedule everything you capture.

STEP 3 Turn a Tossed Thought Into a Reminder

Changed your mind? Find the information you Tossed and schedule a reminder for it later.

You can capture something now without deciding immediately when you'll need it again.

STEP 4 Schedule a Reminder

If something needs to return to your attention at a particular time, schedule a Shout instead of simply Tossing it.

For example, keep the details of an upcoming meeting and arrange to be reminded when they're useful.

STEP 5 Use Your Calendar When Appropriate

Some information belongs on your calendar, such as a meeting at a specific time. Use the calendar option when you need that information in your schedule.

A reminder and a calendar entry serve different purposes. Choose what fits the information.

STEP 6 Receive and Acknowledge Your Shout

When a scheduled Shout arrives, it brings the information back to your attention. Acknowledge it when you've received it.

The goal isn't to keep every thought active. It's to have the information available when you need it.

That's it.

Capture what matters.
Bring it back as a reminder when it matters.
Make room in your brain for whatever comes next.
