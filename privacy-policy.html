MINDBEBOP Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-07-01

1. Introduction

MINDBEBOP (“we”, “our”, “the developer”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all MINDBEBOP applications, including MindEntry, MindEntryBiz, MindFlipOut, MindShoutOut, MindZoneOut, MindBackyard, MindBackOut, and any built-in modes or features released under the MINDBEBOP brand, including time-based thought release functionality.

All MINDBEBOP apps operate entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or transmit any of the following data:

  • Personal information
  • Email address or contact information
  • Location data
  • Identifiers (device ID, advertising ID)
  • Analytics, usage data, diagnostics, or crash logs
  • Contacts, photos, files, or browsing history
  • Information from third-party SDKs

MINDBEBOP apps do not use external servers, cloud storage, or third-party analytics services. All processing happens locally on your device.

When available, some features may use Apple-provided on-device language models. This processing occurs entirely on your device. MINDBEBOP does not receive, collect, store, or transmit this processing.

3. Data You Create

Depending on the app, you may create text entries, written thoughts, responses, reminders, scheduled notifications, audio recordings, recipe-related content, session-related content, favorites, tags, folders, presets, prompt schedules, event-based actions, and app preferences.

All such data:

  • Remains stored only on your device
  • Is not transmitted to MINDBEBOP
  • Is not shared with third parties
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

4. Notifications

Some MINDBEBOP apps use local notifications to deliver reminders, prompts, and recipe triggers. Notification content is created, stored, and delivered entirely on your device.

Notifications may be used for:

  • MindShoutOut reminders
  • Scheduled recipe runs in MindEntry
  • MindPreCog morning, bedtime, and weekly prompts
  • Event-based actions that open MindEntry or other MINDBEBOP apps

No notification content is transmitted to MINDBEBOP.

5. Audio Features

If an app allows audio recording or playback:

  • Audio recordings remain stored only on your device
  • No recordings are uploaded, transmitted, or analyzed externally
  • You may delete audio at any time

6. Inter-App Communication and Cross-Dimensional Handshakes

Some MINDBEBOP apps can optionally communicate with each other using an on-device architecture called Cross-Dimensional Handshakes.

A Cross-Dimensional Handshake represents a structured event between MINDBEBOP apps or between a MINDBEBOP app and supported iOS system features. These handshakes exchange only the minimum information required to continue a requested action.

Cross-Dimensional Handshakes occur entirely on your device using Apple-provided technologies such as custom URL schemes, Share Extensions, App Intents, local notifications, shared on-device storage, or similar on-device communication mechanisms. They do not require MINDBEBOP servers.

Cross-Dimensional Handshakes exchange discrete, user-initiated events rather than continuously sharing information between apps. Each handshake transfers only the information necessary to perform the requested action while keeping all other information on your device.

This may include:

  • Sending thoughts, reminders, reflections, or recipe content between MINDBEBOP apps when you explicitly request it
  • Launching another MINDBEBOP app as part of a Recipe, CogniHack, or manual workflow
  • Cross-app actions such as MindFlipOut → MindZoneOut, MindShoutOut → MindFlipOut, MindZoneOut → MindShoutOut, or similar supported workflows
  • Using Send to Recipe to continue work inside MindEntry
  • Returning from another MINDBEBOP app back into MindEntry while continuing a recipe
  • Opening MindEntry, MindZoneOut, MindBackyard, or other MINDBEBOP apps from notifications or recipe steps
  • Launching MindPreCog prompts and other event-based actions
  • Future Cross-Dimensional Handshakes introduced in later versions that follow the same privacy principles described in this policy

Some handshakes may include text or other content that you explicitly choose to send to another MINDBEBOP app. This content remains on your device and is transferred only to perform the requested action.

All such communication:

  • Occurs entirely on your device
  • Does not require MINDBEBOP servers
  • Does not transmit data outside your device unless you explicitly configure an optional external integration
  • Shares only the minimum information necessary for the requested action
  • Can be avoided simply by not using optional cross-app features

6A. Optional External Integrations

Some versions of MindEntry may support optional external integrations through the MindEvent API or similar event-based interfaces.

These integrations are entirely optional and are initiated only when you explicitly configure them.

When enabled, only the event information necessary for the configured integration is transmitted. MINDBEBOP does not automatically transmit your thoughts. If an optional integration allows user-provided content to be included, only the content you explicitly choose to send is transmitted.

MINDBEBOP does not automatically upload your thoughts to external services. Any external communication occurs only according to the destinations that you configure.

7. No Third-Party Access

MINDBEBOP apps do not use:

  • Analytics frameworks
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • External data services, except optional integrations you explicitly configure

8. Security

Because MINDBEBOP apps do not collect or transmit data, security is handled by your device and Apple’s iOS protections, including encrypted local storage, passcode protection, Touch ID, and Face ID where available.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

As MINDBEBOP evolves, additional Cross-Dimensional Handshakes, on-device integrations, or supported iOS communication mechanisms may be introduced while continuing to follow the privacy principles described in this Privacy Policy.

App-Specific Policies

MindFlipOut

MindFlipOut is a local journaling and thought-response app. All written thoughts, rewrites, and reflections remain stored only on your device.

If Manual Shout or Auto Shout features are enabled and MindShoutOut is installed, the text of a thought response may be shared locally with MindShoutOut. This sharing occurs only on your device and never leaves it.

MindShoutOut

MindShoutOut stores reminder text, scheduled times, optional speech settings, and audio recordings locally on your device. Notifications are generated using iOS local notification services only.

If Manual Zone or Auto Zone features are enabled and MindZoneOut is installed, the text of a reminder related to a thought may be shared locally with MindZoneOut for on-device display.

If you configure the optional “When Tapped” action, tapping a notification may open MindEntry or MindZoneOut. This behavior occurs entirely on-device.

MindZoneOut

MindZoneOut is a local stillness and reflection app. Incoming messages, short reflections, and session-related content are processed and displayed entirely on your device.

If Manual Flip or Auto Flip features are used and MindFlipOut is installed, reflection text may be shared locally with MindFlipOut to continue working with the thought. No data is transmitted outside your device.

MindBackyard

MindBackyard is a local digital fidget environment designed to gently occupy restless attention. All environments, favorites, settings, and optional preferences are stored entirely on your device.

Optional ambient sounds and haptic settings are processed locally and are never transmitted to MINDBEBOP or any third party.

If you use the built-in MindEaseOut release field before entering the Backyard, any text you enter is processed entirely on-device.

MindBackyard does not collect analytics, gameplay data, behavioral data, or any personal information.

MindEaseOut (Time-Based Thought Release function)

All MINDBEBOP apps include a time-based thought release feature that allows a thought to be acknowledged as relating to the past or future.

When used, text entered into this feature:

  • Is processed entirely on-device
  • Is not stored as a history unless explicitly saved by the user
  • Is not transmitted to other apps or external services
  • Is not collected by MINDBEBOP

MindBackOut

MindBackOut is a local session-based app that temporarily blocks selected applications using Apple’s Screen Time system. All configuration and session data are stored entirely on your device.

When you select apps to leave outside, this information is stored locally and used only by Apple’s on-device Screen Time framework to apply temporary restrictions. MINDBEBOP does not receive or access this information.

If you enter text in the “Leave at the Door” field and choose to send it to MindShoutOut, the text is transferred locally between apps using on-device iOS mechanisms. This transfer does not use external servers and does not leave your device.

If the optional “Zone Cooldown” feature is enabled, MindBackOut may automatically open MindZoneOut after a session ends. No data is transmitted externally during this process. The interaction occurs entirely on your device.

Presets that store selected apps, duration, and Zone Cooldown settings are stored entirely on your device.

MindBackOut does not collect:

  • App usage history
  • Screen Time statistics
  • Device activity reports
  • Blocked app names outside your device
  • Any personal or identifying information

MindBackOut uses Apple’s Screen Time APIs (such as FamilyControls and ManagedSettings) solely to apply temporary app restrictions based on your explicit selection. These system frameworks operate entirely on-device, and MINDBEBOP does not access, collect, or receive any usage data from them.

MindEntry

MindEntry is a local routing and recipe app that helps direct thoughts to the appropriate MINDBEBOP tools on your device. Text you enter, recipes you create, saved recipe history, and imported recipe content remain stored entirely on your device.

MindEntry may open other installed MINDBEBOP apps using on-device iOS mechanisms such as custom URL schemes in order to continue a selected recipe step. It may also receive return actions from those apps when a recipe continues. These interactions occur entirely on your device and do not use external servers.

MindEntry may coordinate Cross-Dimensional Handshakes between MINDBEBOP apps in order to continue Recipes, CogniHacks, Manual Backyard, Send to Recipe, and other supported workflows. These interactions occur entirely on your device unless you explicitly configure an optional external integration.

If you import a recipe code, short code, or MINDBEBOP recipe link, the recipe content is processed locally on your device for preview, saving, or execution. MINDBEBOP does not collect, transmit, or store this imported content externally.

If available on your device, MindEntry may use Apple’s on-device language models to help classify text you enter or generate lightweight recipe explanations. This processing occurs entirely on-device and is not transmitted to MINDBEBOP or third parties.

MindEntry may display MindBlank, a passive reflection of how long your mind remained quiet after using MINDBEBOP. This information is calculated entirely on-device and is not transmitted to MINDBEBOP.

MindBlank is not a health score, diagnosis, medical metric, stress score, or emotional analysis.

MindPreCog allows you to schedule morning, bedtime, weekly, and event-based prompts using local notifications. Prompt schedules, associated actions, and related settings are stored entirely on your device.
