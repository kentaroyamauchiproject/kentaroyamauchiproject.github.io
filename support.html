MINDBEBOP is an independent project creating noise-canceling apps for your mind.

♡ Support MINDBEBOP

Donations are voluntary.

Why support MINDBEBOP?

Some thoughts keep returning. Some take up more attention than we would like. Sometimes we know exactly where we want to go, but getting there isn't so simple.

MINDBEBOP explores different ways to work with those moments.

Not by forcing thoughts to disappear, but by giving them places, boundaries, different perspectives, and possible paths.

The goal is simple: help attention move more freely.

What your support makes possible

Your contribution helps support:

Continued development.
Improving existing apps and experimenting with new ways to work with thoughts.

Accessibility.
Keeping as many MINDBEBOP tools freely available as possible.

Independent exploration.
Making room for ideas that may not fit conventional productivity or wellness apps.

Thank you.

Whether you make a donation, use the apps, share them with someone, or simply explore the ideas, your interest means a great deal.

There is no obligation to contribute. MINDBEBOP is here for you to explore.
