MindBackyard

App size: ~3.1 MB
Available on the App Store

MindBackyard is a lo-fi digital fidget for restless attention — a quiet place to move without needing a goal.

Open low-stakes doors, drift through simple corridors, or play with tactile noise while your mind settles in the background.

Designed to occupy your hands without hijacking your attention.

Version 1.2 adds the new “Perspective Room” chamber, expanding the Backyard with another low-stimulation space for restless attention.

What It Is

Not a game. Not a puzzle. Not a goal-oriented activity.

A lo-fi digital fidget designed to catch the excess energy of a restless or overloaded mind. It gives your hands something simple to do without adding another reward loop.

The Lo-Fi Elements

  • Low-Stakes Doors: Open a door and find another. Keep moving without needing to arrive anywhere.
  • Simple Corridors: Move through minimal, repeating architecture at your own pace. No score, no destination, no pressure.
  • Perspective Room: A quiet chamber built around shifting visual perspective, allowing attention to move without needing a task, score, or destination.
  • Tactile Noise: Touch-responsive particles, ripples, and haptics that give your hands something simple to do while your mind settles.

Low-Stimulation by Design

MindBackyard is intentionally simple. It is not built to excite, reward, or keep you hooked.

It offers gentle motion, quiet interaction, and lo-fi spaces for moments when your attention wants movement, not more information.

Like all MINDBEBOP apps, MindBackyard runs entirely on-device with zero tracking.

How It Fits

MindBackyard serves as the "buffer" in your Mental Background OS.

  • After Overload: Use it after a heavy data dump in MindEntry to cool down the brain.
  • Before Focus: Use it for 60 seconds to "park" stray thoughts before activating MindBackOut.
  • During Loops: Use it as a physical circuit breaker for looping thoughts.

Backyard Recipes

You can include MindBackyard as a step in your MindEntry Recipes.

  • Entry → Backyard → Zone (Deep Cool Down)
  • Flip → Backyard (Pattern Break)
  • Backyard → Back (Pre-Focus Anchor)

Mindset: Aimless Has a Place

In the Mental Background OS, we don't always need to "solve" a thought. Sometimes we just need to let it run in a safe, contained backyard until it loses its charge.

MindBackyard provides that container.
