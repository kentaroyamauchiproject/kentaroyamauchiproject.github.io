MindBackyard

App size: ~3.1 MB
Available on the App Store

Your mind wants to move.

It does not always need somewhere important to go.

MindBackyard gives restless attention a quiet place to wander without goals, scores, or another stream of information.

What is MindBackyard?

MindBackyard is a lo-fi digital fidget for moments when your attention wants movement but your mind does not need another task.

Open doors, move through simple corridors, shift perspective, or interact with tactile visual noise.

There is nothing to complete.

How It Works

  1. Enter the Backyard.
  2. Choose a simple environment.
  3. Move, tap, or wander without a goal.
  4. Leave when your attention feels ready for something else.

You do not have to achieve calm. You only give your attention somewhere gentler to go.

Common Moments

You finished one thing but are not ready to begin the next.

Your hands keep reaching for your phone even though you do not want more content.

Your mind feels restless, but thinking harder is not helping.

You need a small buffer between work and rest.

You want to move through something without needing to arrive anywhere.

What MindBackyard Is Not

  • Not a game
  • Not a puzzle
  • Not a productivity tool
  • Not a focus challenge
  • Not an endless content feed

The Backyard Environments

  • Low-Stakes Doors: Open one door and find another. Keep moving without needing to arrive.
  • Simple Corridors: Wander through minimal repeating spaces at your own pace.
  • Perspective Room: Shift the way the space appears without solving or completing anything.
  • Tactile Noise: Tap, touch, and interact with particles, ripples, and subtle haptics.

Low-Stimulation by Design

MindBackyard is intentionally simple.

It is not designed to excite you, reward you, or keep you inside the app.

It offers gentle movement and quiet interaction for moments when your attention wants activity but not more information.

A Buffer Between Mental States

Sometimes the mind does not move directly from one state into another.

Work does not instantly become rest. Overload does not instantly become focus. A looping thought does not instantly become silence.

MindBackyard can sit between those states and give your attention time to shift.

When Wandering Becomes Part of a Route

MindBackyard can also appear inside a MindEntry Recipe or CogniHack.

It may be used as a short buffer before focus, after overload, or between two different mental states.

  • MindEntry → MindBackyard → MindZoneOut
  • MindFlipOut → MindBackyard
  • MindBackyard → MindBackOut

MINDBEBOP calls this Mental Navigation.

Aimless Has a Place

Not every thought needs to be solved. Not every moment needs to become productive.

Sometimes attention simply needs a safe place to move until something changes.

MindBackyard provides that place.

MINDBEBOP Access

The basic MindBackyard experience is free.

MINDBEBOP Access unlocks every available Backyard environment.

Private by Design

MindBackyard runs entirely on your device.

There are no accounts, no behavioral profiles, and no tracking of how you move through the Backyard.
