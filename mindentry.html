MindEntry

App size: ~1.9 MB
Available on the App Store

MindEntry is the entry point to the MINDBEBOP Mental Background OS — where thoughts enter the system.

Write a thought, run a recipe, share a recipe, schedule a recipe, or move directly into the right MINDBEBOP tool.

Version 1.7 improves recipe sharing, Recipe Runner focus, Mark Complete feedback, recipe length, and About organization.

What It Is

Not a chat app. Not a journal. Not a coaching layer.

A simple entry point for routing mental friction — looping thoughts, unfinished tasks, overload, or role pressure — to the right place.

How It Works

  • Type a thought as it appears.
  • MindEntry detects the pattern.
  • It may open a single MINDBEBOP app or suggest a short recipe.
  • If a recipe continues across apps, you can return to MindEntry and move to the next step.

All processing stays on your device. MINDBEBOP does not collect or transmit your thought content.

Common Routes

  • Looping thought → MindFlipOut
  • Unfinished task → MindShoutOut
  • Mental overload → MindZoneOut
  • Role pressure → MindBackOut
  • Restless attention → MindBackyard

Recipes

Some situations need more than one step.

MindEntry can run short mental recipes such as:

  • Schedule → Flip
  • Flip → Zone
  • Back Out → Zone
  • Shout → Backyard

You can create recipes, edit them, share them, import them, and run them across MINDBEBOP apps.

Recipe steps can also include custom titles and detailed instructions, making longer multi-step flows easier to follow while moving between apps.

Recipes can be shared as compact MER1 codes. Shared recipes now preserve full step details and instructions when imported on another device.

Copy a code, paste it into MindEntry, and import the same sequence instantly.

Recipes can now be shared with scannable QR recipe cards and compact MER1 import text for Messages, Mail, and Copy.

Shared step summaries use clearer plain-language descriptions, so recipes are easier to understand outside the app.

You can also share recipes directly from Recent, free.

Recipes can now include up to 10 steps. Shorter recipes, around 3–5 steps, still work best for most moments.

Recipe creation, editing, sharing, importing, and single manual runs are available without purchase. Free users can save up to 10 recipes.

Recipe Organization

Recipes can be organized so frequently used sequences stay easy to find.

  • Favorites keep important recipes near the top.
  • Folders group recipes by situation or routine.
  • Tags make recipes easier to filter and reuse.

    • Recipe search can also match situations, step instructions, tags, and related terms so recipes are easier to rediscover later.

Favorites, folders, and tags are part of MINDBEBOP Access.

Scheduled Recipes

Recipes can be scheduled to return at a specific time.

  • Set a time for a recipe.
  • Choose Once or Daily.
  • Unlock options can appear immediately after previewing or importing locked recipes.
  • When the notification appears, tap it to open MindEntry and run the recipe.

Scheduled recipes use local notifications. They do not run in the background without your tap.

MindShoutOut Trigger

A recipe can also be triggered when returning from a MindShoutOut notification.

For example, a scheduled shout can bring you back to MindEntry, where the selected recipe starts immediately.

This creates a simple time-based entry point without turning MindEntry into a clock or task manager.

Multi-Run

Some recipes work better when repeated.

Multi-run lets a recipe run multiple times in sequence, using the same steps in the same order.

  • Flip → Zone, run 3 times
  • Flip → Zone → Flip → Zone → Flip → Zone

Multi-run uses the existing recipe flow. It does not create loops, branches, or background automation.

Recipe Handshake

When a recipe moves into another MINDBEBOP app, the step can return to MindEntry after completion so the recipe can continue without losing your place.

This happens using on-device iOS mechanisms only.

Recipe Runner

MindEntry can guide you through short mental reset sequences one step at a time.

Instead of remembering what to do next while overwhelmed, frozen, overloaded, or distracted, the Recipe Runner keeps the sequence in front of you.

  • One step at a time
  • Minimal decision-making
  • Designed for overloaded moments

Some recipes use MINDBEBOP apps. Others work as simple cognitive reset flows.

The goal is not productivity or optimization. The goal is reducing cognitive friction so the mind can move again.

MindBlank

MindBlank is a passive reflection of the time your mind stayed quiet after using MINDBEBOP.

It is not a score, streak, diagnosis, or performance metric.

It simply reflects the quiet interval when unresolved loops were not asking for your attention.

You can also generate and share a simple visual summary of your recent MindBlank activity directly from the About screen.

MindPreCog

MindPreCog helps MindEntry appear at moments when background thoughts are likely to return.

  • Morning prompts
  • Bedtime prompts
  • Weekly prompts
  • Event-based prompts
  • Actions that can open MindEntry or other MINDBEBOP tools, including quick Zone resets before events

Calendar-based prompts can open MindZoneOut directly before meetings, work sessions, or social situations for a short quiet reset.

MindPreCog is not prediction or analysis. It is a lightweight prompt system for moments when mental background activity often becomes active again.

On-Device Intelligence

If available on your device, MindEntry may use Apple’s on-device language models to help classify text or generate lightweight recipe explanations.

This processing stays entirely on-device and is not sent to MINDBEBOP or third parties.

Works With MINDBEBOP Apps

MindEntry works together with other MINDBEBOP apps already installed on your device.

  • MindFlipOut for looping thoughts
  • MindShoutOut for unfinished tasks and reminders
  • MindZoneOut for attention reset
  • MindBackOut for stepping out of role pressure
  • MindEaseOut for release
  • MindBackyard for restless attention

If a required app is not installed, MindEntry guides you to install it.

MINDBEBOP Access

MindEntry is free to use for creating, editing, sharing, importing, and running recipes once.

MINDBEBOP Access unlocks additional organization and automation features:

  • Save more than 10 recipes
  • Favorites
  • Folders
  • Tags
  • Scheduled recipe runs
  • Recipe auto-run from MindShoutOut notifications
  • Multi-run support
  • Recent run history
  • Mind Detox recipes published on mindbebop.com
  • AI-assisted routing and explanations when available
