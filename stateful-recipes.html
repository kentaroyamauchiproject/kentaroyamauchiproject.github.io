Stateful Recipes

One destination. Different routes.

Stateful Recipes are guided pathways that help you reach a chosen Point B.

Unlike a fixed recipe, they do not assume that one sequence of steps will work every time.

If your situation changes while the recipe is running, the route can change too.

The destination stays the same. The pathway can adapt.

Whether your Point B is sending an email, having a difficult conversation, preparing a presentation, or reaching a calmer state, the recipe continues guiding you toward the same destination.

Instead of creating a different recipe for every possible situation, one Stateful Recipe can support many different journeys.

Same destination. Different route.

Most recipes follow one fixed sequence of steps. Every run takes the same route.

Stateful Recipes keep the destination the same, but the route can change when needed.

  • The goal stays the same.
  • The next step can adapt to the current moment.
  • Alternative routes can help you continue moving.
  • The journey changes without changing the destination.

One destination. Different routes.

Why This Exists

The first route is not always the best one.

Many systems assume that if a plan is good, following the same steps will always lead to the goal.

But real situations change.

You may hesitate. Lose focus. Get interrupted. Or simply find that the next step feels harder than expected.

That does not mean the destination should change.

It may simply mean a different route is needed.

Stateful Recipes shift the question from:

Why am I stuck?

to:

What is the best way forward from here?

The destination stays the same. Only the route may change.

Fixed Recipes

Fixed Recipes are useful when the same route works every time.

Open the document.
Write the first paragraph.
Save the file.

If every step is predictable, a fixed sequence is often the simplest solution.

But many journeys are less predictable.

You may hesitate. Get interrupted. Lose momentum. Or discover that the next step is harder than expected.

The destination has not changed. But the original route may no longer be the best one.

Stateful Recipes

A Stateful Recipe guides you toward the same destination, even if the original route no longer fits the current moment.

While the recipe is running, it can respond to what is happening now.

It may ask for your current situation, suggest a different route, or continue with the original path if everything is moving smoothly.

Point B stays the same.
The route can change.

Instead of creating a new recipe for every possible situation, one Stateful Recipe can adapt while keeping the same destination.

The journey changes. The goal does not.

Your Situation and Your Step

Providing context while you move.

A Stateful Recipe does not always need every detail before it begins.

When needed, it can ask about your current situation or the next visible step while the journey is already in progress.

This allows one shared recipe to adapt without changing its destination.

Your Situation
What are you avoiding right now?

You answer:

Write the report

Later, the recipe may ask:

Your Step
What is the smallest visible action you can take right now?

You answer:

Open the document

The answers become part of this journey.

One destination. One shared recipe. A route that adapts to the current moment.

Example: The First Move

The First Move is one example of a Stateful Recipe in action.

The destination is simple:

Clean the room

The recipe begins by asking:

What are you avoiding right now?

You answer:

Clean the room

Instead of asking you to complete the entire task, the recipe narrows the route.

What is the smallest visible action you can take right now?

You answer:

Pick up three things

The journey continues from there.

The destination stays the same. The route becomes easier to follow.

Why It Matters

Real journeys rarely unfold exactly as planned.

Even with a clear destination, people hesitate, get interrupted, lose momentum, or discover that the next step feels harder than expected.

A fixed recipe often has only one response:

Follow the next step.

Stateful Recipes offer another possibility:

Continue toward the same destination using a different route.

The destination remains the same.

The journey adapts to the current moment.

Progress does not depend on one perfect sequence.

The Last Responsible Moment

Some decisions are better made when the journey begins, not when the route is created.

A Stateful Recipe may be used today, next week, or months from now.

The destination can stay the same. The current situation may not.

Instead of assuming what the recipe will operate on, it can simply ask when the answer is most useful.

What matters right now?

The answer might be:

  • Write the report
  • Reply to Sarah
  • Clean the room
  • Prepare the presentation
  • Make the phone call

The recipe stays the same. The journey begins with the reality in front of you.
