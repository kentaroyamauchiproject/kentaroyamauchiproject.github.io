MindShoutOut

App size: ~2.4 MB
Available on the App Store — free

You don't want to forget it.

You also don't want to keep thinking about it.

MindShoutOut gives a thought a future place and time so it no longer has to compete for your attention.

MINDBEBOP calls this Thought Noise.

What is MindShoutOut?

MindShoutOut is a place for thoughts that refuse to leave because they feel unfinished.

You write the thought down once and decide what should happen next.

The goal is not to forget the thought. The goal is to stop carrying it.

How It Works

  1. Write the thought.
  2. Choose when it should return.
  3. Release it.
  4. Decide what to do when it comes back.

Sometimes the thought disappears completely. Sometimes it simply stops asking for attention.

Common Examples

Shout

"Remember to send the document tomorrow."

Shout

"Think about this after work."

Shout

"I'll decide this tonight."

Shout

"I'll come back to this in an hour."

What MindShoutOut Is Not

  • Not a task manager
  • Not a productivity system
  • Not journaling
  • Not a calendar replacement
  • Not therapy

When a Shout Isn't Enough

Sometimes a thought needs more than a reminder.

Sometimes it needs a different perspective. Sometimes it needs a route. Sometimes it needs silence.

MindShoutOut can send a thought into MindFlipOut, MindEntry, MindZoneOut, or MindEaseOut.

MINDBEBOP calls this Mental Navigation.

Features

MINDBEBOP Integration

MindShoutOut can send a Shout directly into MindEntry for additional handling options.

A returning thought can also open MindEntry or MindZoneOut when a notification banner is tapped.

This allows a thought to move naturally between scheduling, routing, Recipe workflows, cooling down in Zone, or taking a practical first step.

All integrations are optional. MindShoutOut can still be used as a standalone scheduling tool.

Send to Recipe

Shouts can be sent directly into compatible MindEntry Recipes.

A thought can move naturally from scheduling and storage into a structured sequence when needed.

Recipes may include Stateful Recipes, CogniHacks, Hallway Mode, or other MindEntry flows.

Send to Recipe is optional. Many thoughts still begin and end inside MindShoutOut.

Manual Flip

Shouts can be sent directly to MindFlipOut.

Sometimes a thought does not need another reminder. It needs a different perspective.

Manual Flip allows a stored thought to move directly into a Heavy → Light response.

Manual Flip complements scheduling, Zone integration, Recipe integration, and MindEntry workflows.

Private by Design

Your thoughts remain on your device.

MindShoutOut is designed to help you release a thought, not collect it.
