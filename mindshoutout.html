MindShoutOut

App size: ~2.1 MB
Available on the App Store — free

MindShoutOut is a scheduling and handoff tool for thoughts that feel too important to carry in the mind.

It gives persistent thoughts a future moment so they no longer require continuous attention.

Version 1.8 adds deeper MindEntry integration, including the ability to send any Shout directly into MindEntry for additional handling options and a new “Take the First Step” workflow for tasks that feel difficult to begin. It also improves cross-app flow between MindShoutOut and MindEntry, updates About information, and includes stability improvements and fixes.

What It Is

Not a productivity system. Not a task manager.

A structured method for scheduling or storing a single thought.

The purpose is to give an unfinished thought a place and a time, so it does not keep asking for attention in the background.

Common Uses

  • Write down a thought that feels too important to forget and schedule it to return later as a notification.
  • Use Toss to quickly park a thought without turning it into a notification.
  • Set a far future reminder and keep it stable even when Repeat is enabled.
  • Record an Audio Shout with an editable label.
  • Send a thought (Shout) or an Audio Shout label to MindZoneOut for soft and fuzzy whisper display on the blank wall.
  • Send a Shout directly to MindEntry for additional handling options.
  • Use Take the First Step when a task feels difficult to begin.
  • Open MindEntry from a notification banner so the returning thought can start inside the Mental Background OS.

Used when a heavy thought that feels too important to forget repeatedly interrupts focus.

MindEntry Integration

MindShoutOut can send a Shout directly into MindEntry for additional handling options.

A returning thought can also open MindEntry or MindZoneOut when a notification banner is tapped.

This allows a thought to move naturally between scheduling, routing, Recipe workflows, cooling down in Zone, or taking a practical first step.

All integrations are optional. MindShoutOut can still be used as a standalone scheduling tool.
