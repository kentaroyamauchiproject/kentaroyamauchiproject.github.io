Cross-Dimensional Handshake

A bidirectional connection between the real world and the Mental Background Layer.

Most MINDBEBOP tools exist entirely inside the Mental Background Layer.

Thoughts can be typed directly into MindEntry or shared from other apps using the Share Sheet. These intentional thought inputs begin inside your awareness.

A Cross-Dimensional Handshake is different.

It allows meaningful events to cross the boundary between reality and the Mental Background Layer in both directions.

Reality can initiate mental state movement inside MINDBEBOP, and completed mental transitions can quietly notify the outside world.

From Reality to the Mental Background Layer

Some moments begin outside MINDBEBOP.

A meeting ends. You arrive home. Focus Mode starts. A Shortcut runs. A future webhook arrives.

These events can become MindEvents, allowing MindEntry to respond with an appropriate Recipe or CogniHack while you remain completely in control.

What Is Shared

Cross-Dimensional Handshakes exchange events, not thoughts.

Incoming handshakes describe something that happened in the real world.

Outgoing handshakes announce that a chosen mental transition has completed.

Your thoughts, conversations, and internal mental state remain inside the Mental Background Layer unless you deliberately choose otherwise.

Why It Exists

Life continuously creates moments that influence the mind.

Likewise, completed mental transitions often lead naturally to actions in the real world.

The Cross-Dimensional Handshake allows those two worlds to acknowledge each other without turning MINDBEBOP into an automation platform.

What It Is Not

  • Not task automation
  • Not artificial intelligence
  • Not a workflow builder
  • Not a productivity system
  • Not an integration platform
  • Not another place to configure your life

Automation happens outside MINDBEBOP. MINDBEBOP simply receives meaningful events and announces completed mental transitions.

Current Implementation

Cross-Dimensional Handshake is an architectural concept, not a specific technology.

Today, MINDBEBOP performs Cross-Dimensional Handshakes using technologies such as URL schemes, App Intents, and standard webhooks.

Future implementations may use entirely different mechanisms while preserving the same principle: allowing meaningful events to cross the boundary between reality and the Mental Background Layer.

Why It Matters

MINDBEBOP does not attempt to control reality.

It creates a gentle bridge between reality and the Mental Background Layer.

Reality can influence mental state movement, and completed mental state movement can quietly influence reality.

Thoughts stay inside the Mental Background Layer.

Events can cross its boundary.

That boundary is the Cross-Dimensional Handshake.
