Cross-Dimensional Handshake

When a completed mental transition quietly reaches the real world.

Most MINDBEBOP tools exist entirely inside the Mental Background Layer.

Thoughts are flipped, shouted, released, protected, or guided through recipes without ever leaving that space.

A Cross-Dimensional Handshake is different.

It allows a completed recipe to quietly notify the outside world that something meaningful has happened.

From the Mental Background Layer to Reality

MINDBEBOP does not automate your life.

It simply announces that a mental transition has been completed.

What happens next is entirely your choice.

Mental Background Layer ↓ Recipe Completed ↓ Cross-Dimensional Handshake ↓ Real World

Examples

  • Turn on smart lights
  • Start a playlist
  • Run an Apple Shortcut
  • Update a Slack status
  • Log to Google Sheets
  • Call a custom API
  • Trigger Home Assistant
  • Anything your automation platform supports

MINDBEBOP never decides what should happen. It simply sends a quiet signal that your recipe has completed.

What Is Shared

Only the completion of a recipe is shared.

Not your thoughts.

Not your mental state.

Not your conversations.

Just the fact that a chosen transition has completed.

Why It Exists

MINDBEBOP helps reduce the amount of thought residue carried into reality.

Sometimes that completed transition naturally leads to an action outside MINDBEBOP.

The Cross-Dimensional Handshake simply makes that possible.

What It Is Not

  • Not task automation
  • Not artificial intelligence
  • Not a workflow builder
  • Not a productivity system
  • Not an integration platform
  • Not another place to configure your life

Automation happens outside MINDBEBOP. MINDBEBOP simply announces completion.

Current Implementation

Today, Cross-Dimensional Handshakes are implemented using standard webhooks.

This allows services such as Zapier, Make, n8n, Apple Shortcuts, Home Assistant, and many others to respond however you choose.

Future implementations may include additional ways to perform the same handshake.

Why It Matters

MINDBEBOP does not change reality.

It changes the amount of thought residue carried into reality.

What reality becomes after that is always personal.

A thought remains inside the Mental Background Layer. A completed mental transition can quietly shake hands with the real world.
