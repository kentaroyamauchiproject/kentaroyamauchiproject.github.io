Cross-Dimensional Handshake

A bidirectional connection between the real world and the Mental Background Layer.

Most MINDBEBOP tools exist entirely inside the Mental Background Layer. Thoughts can be typed directly into MindEntry or shared intentionally from other apps using the Share Sheet.

A Cross-Dimensional Handshake is different.

It allows meaningful events to cross the boundary between reality and the Mental Background Layer in both directions.

Reality can initiate mental state movement inside MINDBEBOP, and completed mental transitions can quietly notify the outside world.

The Bidirectional Circle

Reality-driven events become MindEvents inside MindEntry. Completed Recipes and CogniHacks can, in turn, emit structured events back into the real world.

Incoming: Reality → MindEntry

Reality can securely initiate mental state movement without requiring manual navigation inside MindEntry.

Today's Cross-Dimensional Handshake supports multiple ways for meaningful events to enter the Mental Background Layer.

  • Share Extension — Share selected text or thought residue directly into MindEntry.
  • URL Schemes — Launch MindEntry through deep links.
  • App Intents — Trigger MindEntry through Apple's automation framework.
  • Apple Shortcuts — Send MindEvents from calendars, Focus Modes, locations, NFC tags, or custom Shortcut workflows.
  • Secure Webhooks (MindEvent API) — External systems such as Zapier, Make, n8n, Home Assistant, servers, or custom software can securely deliver MindEvents into MindEntry.

Every incoming event becomes a MindEvent. MindEntry may suggest an appropriate Recipe or CogniHack, but nothing runs automatically without user confirmation.

Outgoing: MindEntry → Reality

When a meaningful mental transition completes, MindEntry can securely emit a structured event to the outside world.

The application simply announces that a transition has completed. It does not decide what should happen next.

  • Turn on smart lights
  • Start a playlist
  • Run an Apple Shortcut
  • Update a Slack status
  • Log to Google Sheets
  • Call a custom API
  • Trigger Zapier, Make, n8n, or Home Assistant
  • Anything your automation platform supports

The Firewall Principle

Cross-Dimensional Handshakes exchange structured events—not thoughts.

Incoming handshakes describe something that happened in reality. Outgoing handshakes announce that a meaningful mental transition has completed.

Only event metadata crosses the boundary. Thoughts, reflections, conversations, and internal mental state remain inside the Mental Background Layer unless you deliberately choose otherwise.

Reality influences mental state movement.

Mental state movement influences reality.

Current Implementation

Cross-Dimensional Handshake is an architectural concept rather than a specific technology.

Current implementations include Share Extension, URL Schemes, App Intents, Apple Shortcuts, Universal Links, and the MindEvent API using secure webhooks.

Future implementations may introduce additional transports while preserving the same underlying principle: allowing meaningful events—not thoughts—to cross the boundary between reality and the Mental Background Layer.

Why It Matters

MINDBEBOP does not attempt to automate life.

It provides a secure, structured boundary where reality and the Mental Background Layer can acknowledge one another without exposing private thoughts or removing user choice.

Thoughts stay inside the Mental Background Layer.

Events can cross its boundary.

That boundary is the Cross-Dimensional Handshake.
