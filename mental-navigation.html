Mental Navigation

Moving from Point A to Point B.

MINDBEBOP is not about becoming more productive.

It is about noticing where your mind is stuck, then helping you move toward the mental state you actually want next.

We call this Mental Navigation.

Point A → Point B

Point A is where you are now.

Point B is the mental state you want next.

A Recipe or CogniHack helps you move between them.

Point A

↓

Recipe / CogniHack

↓

Point B

What Point B Can Look Like

Point A Point B
Waiting for a reply Back to what you were doing
Unable to start The first small step
Pre-presentation nerves Ready to begin
Overthinking A quieter mind
Doomscrolling Focused again
Mentally overloaded One clear next move

Why It Is Not Productivity

Productivity tools usually ask what you completed.

MINDBEBOP asks where your mind is stuck, and where you want it to go next.

Point B is not a goal, score, streak, or performance target.

It may be focus. It may be calm. It may be starting. It may be stopping. It may be rest.

You choose Point B.

How MINDBEBOP Helps

Different MINDBEBOP tools support different kinds of movement.

  • MindFlipOut helps loosen a stuck interpretation.
  • MindShoutOut moves a thought out of the present moment and into a chosen time.
  • MindZoneOut creates a quiet space when the mind needs less input.
  • MindBackOut protects attention from outside pulls.
  • MindBackyard offers a low-stimulation place to stay without pressure.
  • MindEntry routes you through Recipes and CogniHacks toward Point B.

The Simple Idea

When your mind gets stuck,

MINDBEBOP helps you move from Point A to Point B.
