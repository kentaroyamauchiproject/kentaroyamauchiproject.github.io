MindEntryBiz

Organizational support layer for the MINDBEBOP Mental Background OS
Enterprise availability coming soon

MindEntryBiz is the organizational companion to the MINDBEBOP Mental Background OS.

It helps organizations support everyday mental hygiene through private participation records, aggregate activity patterns, and optional rewards.

What It Is

Not employee surveillance. Not mood scoring. Not content monitoring.

A privacy-first organizational layer that supports everyday Mental Navigation across the MINDBEBOP ecosystem while keeping thoughts private.

What Stays Private

MindEntryBiz is built around a clear separation between private content and shared participation.

Organizations can support mental hygiene without seeing the contents of a person's mind.

Category Shared with organization
Thought content No
Journal entries No
Mental Steps and CogniHacks No
MindAnchors No
Participation counts Optional
Aggregate trends Yes
Reward activity Optional

The goal is encouragement, not surveillance.

How It Works

  • Members use MINDBEBOP apps normally.
  • MindEntryBiz records non-content activity such as app actions, completed actions, participation frequency, and participation in supported flows.
  • Members can view their own participation and point history.
  • Organizations can view aggregate participation trends and optional reward activity.

Thought contents are not collected or transmitted. MINDBEBOP does not report what users wrote, flipped, scheduled, or released.

What Can Be Tracked

  • Number of flips completed in MindFlipOut
  • Number of reminders or shouts scheduled in MindShoutOut
  • Total Zone time completed in MindZoneOut
  • Backroom session activity in MindBackOut
  • Recipe completion or cross-app flow participation
  • Participation frequency over time

This is behavioral usage data only — not thought content, emotional interpretation, or mental health diagnosis.

What Is Not Tracked

  • No thought contents
  • No journal text
  • No “stress score” or “burnout score”
  • No emotional classification
  • No hidden analysis of what users write

MindAnchor Privacy

MindAnchors are designed to preserve moments of successful Mental Navigation.

MindAnchors remain private by default and are never shared with organizations unless a member explicitly chooses to share them.

Organizations can support participation in mental hygiene practices without gaining access to personal reflections, photographs, or other private materials.

Private for Members

Each member can maintain a private record of participation across supported MINDBEBOP actions.

  • Personal usage history
  • Total activity counts
  • Zone minutes
  • Points earned through optional programs

The system is designed so that mental hygiene remains personal, even when organizations choose to support it.

For Organizations

MindEntryBiz gives organizations a lightweight way to support mental hygiene without intruding into private thought content.

  • Aggregate participation rate
  • Total app usage trends
  • Total Zone time across a team or organization
  • Reward redemption activity
  • Participation growth over time

Designed for organizational encouragement, not individual psychological evaluation.

Optional Rewards

Organizations may choose to offer small rewards for participation in mental hygiene routines.

Examples include:

  • Coffee gift cards
  • Team wellness perks
  • Participation-based point exchanges

Rewards are based on participation, not on emotional outcomes or mental-state judgments.

Works With MINDBEBOP Apps

MindEntryBiz works with the MINDBEBOP ecosystem as an organizational reporting and participation layer.

  • MindFlipOut for looping thoughts
  • MindShoutOut for unfinished tasks and reminders
  • MindZoneOut for attention reset and quiet time
  • MindBackOut for stepping out of role pressure

Organizations can encourage use across the system while keeping the contents of each user’s mental activity private.

Privacy Model

MindEntryBiz is built around a strict separation between content and activity.

  • Thought content stays private
  • Only selected activity metrics are reported
  • Organizations see participation, not private mental material
  • The goal is encouragement, not surveillance

A Different Kind of Organizational Layer

Most workplace wellness systems either ask for self-disclosure or produce vague engagement metrics.

MindEntryBiz focuses instead on small, real mental hygiene actions across the MINDBEBOP Mental Background OS.

MINDBEBOP introduces a modular architecture for structuring the Mental Background layer — and MindEntryBiz extends that architecture into organizational support.
