MindBackOut

App size: ~1.6 MB
Available on the App Store — free

MindBackOut is an ultralight backroom for stepping away from distraction, expectation, and automatic habits

You choose which apps to leave outside. While you’re inside, those apps are blocked.

Version 1.4 adds First Move Unlock, allowing selected apps to remain locked until you complete The First Move (CogniHack recipe) in MindEntry. It also improves the transition from distraction to action through deeper MindEntry integration, improves cross-app workflow, and includes stability improvements and fixes.

What It Is

Not productivity. Not a timer hack. Not a self-control game.

A simple boundary: leave selected apps outside and step into a quiet space where nothing is required.

How It Works

  • Choose a duration (or select None for no timer).
  • Select the apps you want to leave outside (they will be blocked).
  • Enter the backroom.
  • Exit whenever you want — or let the timer end.

MindBackOut uses Apple’s Screen Time controls on-device. MINDBEBOP does not collect or transmit your data.

Presets

Save your preferred Backroom configurations as reusable Presets.

Each Preset stores:

  • Selected apps to lock
  • Session duration
  • Zone Cooldown on or off

This makes it easy to return to the same boundary setup with a single tap.

First Move Unlock

Some distractions are not the problem. Avoiding the first step is.

With First Move Unlock, selected apps remain locked until you complete The First Move (CogniHack recipe) in MindEntry.

The goal is not productivity. The goal is simply to help you move from avoidance to action by taking one small concrete step.

Unlock happens after The First Move is completed in MindEntry.

MindEntry Integration

MindBackOut can work together with MindEntry when stepping away is not enough.

Instead of immediately returning to blocked apps, you can move directly into a structured first-action workflow before unlocking.

This is optional. MindBackOut continues to work as a standalone backroom.

Common Uses

  • Stop automatic reopening of high-frequency apps.
  • Create a clean break before returning to work or messages.
  • Leave social apps outside for a short reset.
  • Drop a reminder at the door (send text to MindShoutOut) before entering.
  • Release active residue with MindEaseOut before entering.
  • Keep distracting apps locked until you complete The First Move in MindEntry.
  • Create a boundary between avoidance and action without relying on willpower alone.

Release Residue (MindEaseOut)

If something still feels active before you enter, you can release it first.

MindEaseOut is an on-device release mode built into MINDBEBOP apps — a quiet passage for setting a thought aside without deleting or analyzing it.

Leave It at the Door (MindShoutOut)

If something is too important to forget but you don’t want to carry it inside, write it down at the door.

MindBackOut can send that text to MindShoutOut as a local reminder — on-device only.

Zone Cooldown (Optional)

If you feel pressure right after leaving the backroom, enable Zone Cooldown.

When enabled, MindBackOut sends you directly into a MindZoneOut Zone session in None mode after exit — so you can cool down before returning to everything.

You choose when to exit the Zone.
