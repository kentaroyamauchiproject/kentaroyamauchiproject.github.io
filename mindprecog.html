MindPreCog

A quiet prompt before thought friction takes hold.

MindPreCog is the context-aware layer of MINDBEBOP.

It notices meaningful moments — when a thought returns, a meeting is about to begin, or a demanding context is approaching — and offers the right structural pathway.

Instead of waiting for you to realize you need help, MindPreCog gently surfaces the right tool at the right time.

No analysis. No interpretation. No diagnosis. Just a timely invitation to reduce thought friction before it builds.

Most apps wait for you to ask for help. MindPreCog offers structure before you think to ask.

  • A stored thought returns as a reminder.
  • A calendar event is about to begin.
  • A period of focus is about to start.
  • A familiar loop begins to reappear.

These moments become quiet opportunities to respond differently.

How MindPreCog Works

Context-aware prompts, not mind reading.

MindPreCog does not analyze your thoughts.

It responds to simple signals such as:

  • A MindShoutOut reminder returning
  • An upcoming calendar event
  • The end of a Backroom session
  • A time-based prompt such as bedtime

When one of these moments occurs, MindEntry can open and offer the most relevant next step.

That might mean flipping a thought, scheduling it, entering Backroom, or simply doing nothing.

Example

You have a meeting in 10 minutes.

MindEntry opens and asks:

Anything you want to leave at the door?

You choose: MindShoutOut, MindFlipOut, or MindBackOut.

A small amount of mental friction is removed before the meeting begins.

What MindPreCog Is Not

  • Not mind reading
  • Not emotional analysis
  • Not artificial intelligence therapy
  • Not a diagnosis
  • Not behavioral scoring
  • Not surveillance
  • Not a prediction of what you are thinking

MindPreCog simply notices meaningful moments and offers structure when it may be useful.

Current Examples

  • Returned Thoughts: A MindShoutOut reminder opens MindEntry when tapped.
  • Calendar Prompts: MindEntry can appear before an upcoming event.

Over time, MindPreCog may expand to include additional context-aware prompts.

Why It Matters

Most mental tools are reactive.

You notice a problem, then decide what to do.

MindPreCog shortens that gap.

It offers a structural option just before thought friction has a chance to grow.

MindPreCog does not try to understand your mind. It simply arrives a little earlier.
