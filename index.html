Noise-canceling apps for your mind

MINDBEBOP helps with thoughts that keep running in the background — and helps your mind move when you want to go somewhere next.

  • 📱 You just checked your phone.
    And your hand is already reaching again.
  • 🛏️ You're trying to sleep.
    Then one old memory starts replaying.
  • 💬 You're waiting for a reply.
    So part of your mind stays attached to it.
  • 🌀 You know what to do.
    But starting feels weirdly unavailable.

MINDBEBOP is a small set of tools for thoughts that would otherwise keep asking for your attention. You can quiet them, give them another time or place, look at them differently, or move toward somewhere you want to be next.

You do not have to process every thought. Sometimes changing what happens next is enough.

Reduce. Navigate. Anchor.

MINDBEBOP tools can help in three broad ways.

Reduce what keeps asking for attention.

Navigate when there is somewhere you want to go.

Anchor something from an arrival worth remembering.

These are possibilities, not required stages. A thought may only need one of them. Or none at all.

Reduce: quiet what keeps asking for attention

Some thoughts keep coming back into the foreground.

A reminder you keep rehearsing. A conversation you keep replaying. A reply you keep checking for. A distraction you keep returning to.

Different thoughts may need different things: another perspective, another time, less stimulation, temporary distance, or simply somewhere else for your attention to move.

Sometimes that is enough.

Navigate: when you want your mind to go somewhere

Sometimes the problem is not what needs to be done. Your mind simply is not where you want it to be yet.

You may be home while part of your mind is still at work. You may know exactly what to do while starting still feels unavailable. Or you may be at Point A and want to move toward a Point B.

Mental Navigation helps you move from your Current Position toward a Mental Destination you choose.

MindEntry can guide that movement through Mental Steps, one step at a time. A step may happen inside a MINDBEBOP app or in the real world.

You choose Point B. The route can change along the way.

Thought Space: give a thought somewhere to be

A thought does not have to become a task.

In MindBackyard, Thought Space lets you turn a selected thought into a persistent Thought Block and place it in space.

You can approach it, move it, rotate it, change your perspective, or leave it alone.

A Thought Block can also connect to one or more possible Mental Steps. If you later decide there is somewhere you want to go with that thought, a possible path is already nearby.

A thought can have a place before it has a destination.

A possible path is not an obligation.

Explore Thought Space →

Anchor: keep something from where you arrived

Reaching Point B does not mean a thought has to disappear.

When a Mental Navigation ends somewhere meaningful, MindAnchor lets you preserve something from that arrival.

It is not a score or proof that something has been resolved. It is simply something you chose to keep.

Not another To Do app

A To Do app helps you keep track of what needs to be done.

MINDBEBOP helps when knowing what to do isn't enough.

Maybe a thought keeps replaying. Maybe you're still waiting for something. Maybe your attention keeps drifting back to your phone. Maybe the task is already on your To Do list — but you still cannot begin.

MINDBEBOP is not a replacement for your To Do app. It works on a different problem: what is happening with your attention and thoughts around the things in your life.
