How to Use MindFlipOut

Take a recurring, negative thought and rewrite it into something lighter or more acceptable to you. Responding to or reframing a thought may help it stop asking for your attention. If it comes back, try Remix to explore another response or perspective.

STEP 1

Enter a Heavy Thought

Select Heavy, type one recurring thought that's bothering you, and tap Enter.

STEP 2

Switch to Light

Tap the Light tab. Your original Heavy thought is already there. Tap the thought to write a lighter response.

STEP 3

Write a Light Response

Rewrite your Heavy thought into something lighter or more acceptable to you. Tap Save when you're ready.

STEP 4

View Your Light Thought

Select Light to see your rewritten thought. You can revisit it whenever you want.

That's it.

One thought at a time.
Flip it when you're ready.
