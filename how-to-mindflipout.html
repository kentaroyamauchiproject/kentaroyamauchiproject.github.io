How to Use MindFlipOut

Take a recurring, negative thought and rewrite it into something lighter or more positive to you. Once it's been responded and/or reframed, it naturally stops asking for attention. If it comes back, try Remixes until it stops.

STEP 1

Enter a Heavy Thought

Select Heavy, type one recurring thought that's bothering you, and tap Enter.

STEP 2

Find Your Heavy Thought

Your thought appears in the Heavy list. Tap it to open the editing screen.

STEP 3

Write a Light Response

Rewrite your Heavy thought into something lighter or more acceptable to you. Tap Save when you're ready.

STEP 4

View Your Light Thought

Select Light to see your rewritten thought. You can revisit it whenever you want.

That's it.

One thought at a time.
Flip it when you're ready.
